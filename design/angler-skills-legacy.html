🎣 Angler Skill Tree

Fish Farm Festival — Player Guide

How it works

1Fish or fightCatching fish (and killing mobs) earns Angler XP.
2Level upFill the XP bar at the top of the Skills screen. Every level = 1 skill point.
3Press K & spendOpen Skills, click a skill to spend points and unlock it.
4Go deeperDeeper skills unlock only after you've spent enough points in the tree.

Reading a skill

❤️ health more hearts 👟 speed move faster 🍀 luck better fishing loot 🛡️ armor tougher 🌊 water underwater power 🎁 loot one-time item reward
Each skill costs a number of points to buy, and many require a minimum number of points already spent in this tree before they appear. Hover any locked skill in-game to see exactly what it needs.
