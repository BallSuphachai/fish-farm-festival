# 🎣 Fish Farm Festival

**A cozy world of fishing, farming and cooking — wrapped around four living seasonal festivals, and an adventure that actually feeds back into your kitchen.**

Fish Farm Festival is a hand-tuned Fabric 1.20.1 modpack for players who love the quiet loop of casting a line, tending a farm and cooking a good meal — but who still want a reason to sharpen a sword now and then. Every system here talks to every other one. The fish you catch become festival prizes. The illagers you fight drop the bait for the fish you *can't* catch yet. The dishes you master keep you alive on the water. Nothing is a side mod bolted on and forgotten.

Play solo at your own pace, or open it up and enjoy the festivals with friends.

---

## 🌊 Cast a Line

Hundreds of fish live in these waters, from the humble bluegill to legendary sharks, koi and glowing deep-sea oddities. Upgrade from a wooden pole to prismarine and golden rods, tie on rare hooks and lines, and brew special baits that bend luck in your favour. Keep your catches in a fish satchel, show off the prettiest ones in home aquariums, or turn them straight over to the cook.

## 🌱 Work the Land

A full cottage-farm stack — Farmer's Delight, Farm & Charm, Expanded Delight, Vinery, Brewery and more — gives you dozens of crops, orchards, vineyards and animals to raise. Grow strawberries and grapes, press juice, brew drinks, tap a stove and fill a cooking pot. **Seeds are precious** — apart from a few wild plants like vanilla crops and beach coconuts, no farm crop grows wild here, so every new one is something you bought at the **Town Market**, traded for at a festival, found in a village or won from an illager. Your farm grows one discovery at a time.

## 🍲 Cook Something Worth Eating

Food isn't just fuel here. **Every mod dish grants a buff**, and the harder the recipe, the longer it lasts:

| Dish type | Buff |
|---|---|
| Seafood | Luck + Water Breathing |
| Hearty stews & roasts | Strength + Resistance |
| Sweet treats | Speed + Haste |
| A full Feast | Luck, Strength, Resistance & Regeneration |

A well-stocked kitchen turns a good angler into an unstoppable one.

---

## 🌳 Three Paths to Master

Pick up the **Angler**, **Farmer** and **Chef** skill trees and grow them your way. The first steps hand you the tools of the trade — an enchanted rod, a chef's kit, a Market stall, rare saplings — while the deep branches hold the real power: Captain's Call for the sailor, Season's Blessing for the farmer, Spice Master and Banquet Host for the cook. A full tree is a years-long journey, and once it's done every extra level drops a **Mastery Crate**. There's always a next thing worth unlocking.

## 🎉 The Festivals

This is the heart of the pack. Four festivals follow the seasons, each running a generous **16 days**, and a **festival merchant comes to you** when one begins — no hunting them down, and a chat button to call them back if you wander off.

- 🌸 **Blossom Fair** — spring
- 🎣 **Grand Fishing Derby** — summer
- 🍂 **Harvest Feast** — autumn
- 🎄 **Christmas** — winter, complete with Santa, Jingle the Elf, snowfall on Christmas Eve and a gift under every player's name on Christmas morning

Hand in your seasonal crops, fish and dishes for **Festival Tokens**, then spend them on hundreds of themed goods and decorations. And every time the merchant appears, keep an eye out for **Festival Treasure** — one or two legendary deals you can *only* get here: a Nether Star, a Netherite Upgrade Template, a Totem of Undying, max-level enchanted books and more. No competitions, no losers — just the season, the stall and the rewards.

## ⚔️ Adventure That Matters

Fighting isn't a separate game over in the corner. Tougher-than-vanilla illagers now storm pillager outposts, mansions, **Dungeons & Taverns** structures and the mighty **Illager Warship**, in every variant the pack can throw at you. Beat the strong ones and they drop what your peaceful side actually needs:

- 🪝 Special **Tide baits** — Primal Essence for legendary fish, Illusionary and Unusual Dust for luck and bite speed
- 🌾 Rare seeds, rare hooks, and **Festival Tokens** out of season
- 📦 Bonus loot tucked into tavern and dungeon chests

Take a **Bounty Board** contract, sail out on a **Small Ship**, and bring the spoils home to your rods and your cooking pot.

### 🚀 Getting Started

1. Install the pack and start a **new world** (required — the pack ships with fresh world generation and structures).
2. Fish, farm or cook — pick whatever calls to you first.
3. Craft a **Market**, open your **FFF Journey** guide, and wait for your first festival to roll in.

Grab your rod. The first festival is closer than you think. 🎣🎄
