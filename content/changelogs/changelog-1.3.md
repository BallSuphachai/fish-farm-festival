# Fish Farm Festival 1.3 — The Festival Update

> ⚠️ **Start a new world.** 1.3 removes Terralith and Let's Do Bakery and adds new structures and systems. Old worlds will have broken terrain and missing blocks.

## 🎄 Seasonal Festivals
- Four festivals follow the Serene Seasons calendar, each lasting 16 days: **Blossom Fair** (spring), **Grand Fishing Derby** (summer), **Harvest Feast** (autumn) and **Christmas** (winter).
- A festival merchant comes to you when a festival starts. If you lose track of them, click **[Call the merchant]** in chat.
- Hand in seasonal crops, fish and dishes for **Festival Tokens**. Spend Tokens on hundreds of themed items and decorations.
- **Festival Treasure:** each visit, the merchant has 1–2 legendary deals available only from festival merchants: Nether Star, Netherite Upgrade Template, Totem, max-level enchanted books and more.
- **Christmas extras:** Santa and Jingle the Elf, Jingle Bells, snow on Christmas Eve, and a gift for every player on Christmas morning.
- A new **Festival Hall of Fame** advancement tab tracks your festivals.

## 🛒 Town Market
- Craft a **Market** (planks, red wool, logs) to buy things with emeralds all year round.
- Tabs: seeds, saplings, animals and pets, fishing and aquarium gear, kitchen tools, building blocks, Small Ships boats, horse gear and enchanted books.
- Seeds and saplings are deliberately expensive, since one purchase lets you replant forever.
- Backpack slots, augment bays and extra equip slots are now unlocked with **emeralds** instead of XP.

## 🌳 Skill Trees Reworked
- Stats are rebalanced: total bonus health is now +20 (was +58) and total armor is +8 (was +28).
- Nodes now give perks that fit each tree:
  - **Angler:** Night Angler, Captain's Call.
  - **Farmer:** Bountiful Harvest crates, Seed Saver, Early Riser, Market Regular.
  - **Chef:** Seasoned Cook, Spice Master, Banquet Host and more.
- **Mastery:** once a tree is fully unlocked, every extra level gives a Mastery Crate. Claim it with the link in chat.
- Chef XP now comes only from mod dishes. Harder recipes give more XP.

## 🍲 Food Buffs
Every mod dish gives a buff. Harder recipes last longer, and feasts give the biggest buff:

| Dish type | Buff |
|---|---|
| Seafood | Luck + Water Breathing |
| Hearty (stews, meat) | Strength + Resistance |
| Sweet | Speed + Haste |
| Feast | Luck, Strength, Resistance and Regeneration |

## ⚔️ Adventure Matters
- Illager Invasion mobs now appear in pillager outposts, mansions, Dungeons & Taverns illager structures, and on the **Illager Warship**. Illagers are tougher than before.
- Stronger illagers drop better loot: special fishing baits, rare hooks, Festival Tokens and rare seeds.
- Some Illager drops work as **Tide bait**:
  - Primal Essence: +4 Luck, for legendary fish.
  - Illusionary Dust and Unusual Dust: Luck and bite-speed bonuses.
- Chests in Dungeons & Taverns can contain bonus FFF loot.
- New **Town Guard** bounty decree.

## 📜 Quality of Life & Fixes
- New **FFF Journey** tab guides new players through every system.
- Bounties last 40 minutes longer.
- Hybrid Aquatic fish now count for bounties, Angler XP, villager trades and the Fishing Derby.
- Duplicate ingredients can be converted at the stonecutter.
- Mod crops now follow Serene Seasons.
- FPS: nearby water-mob counts are capped, and Dynamic Lights is tuned for performance.
- Removed Terralith, Let's Do Bakery and Hunger's Delight.
