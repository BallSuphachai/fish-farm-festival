# Fish Farm Festival 1.3.1 — Seeds Are Precious

> ⚠️ **Start a new world.** Wild crops that already generated in old worlds stay there, and your unlocked skill nodes will move to new positions.

## 🌱 No More Wild Crops
- Mod crops and most fruit trees no longer grow in the wild. This covers all Farmer's Delight, Farm & Charm, Expanded Delight, Brewery and Snowy Spirit wild crops, Vinery grape bushes and apple and cherry trees, and walnut and cinnamon trees.
- Vanilla plants still grow in their usual biomes: sugar cane, pumpkins, melons and sweet berries. Coconut palms still grow on beaches.
- Every mod seed you plant now has to be earned. You can buy seeds at the Market or from festival merchants, win them from illagers, or get them from Harvest Crates.
- Wheat seeds still drop from grass, so a new player can always start a farm.
- The Market now also sells corn kernels, Farm & Charm onions and all six rare grape varieties.
- Farmer's Delight crops no longer show up in village farms or village chests.
- Removed **Farmers Structures**. Its houses were full of free crop crates and fields.

## 🌾 Farmer XP Can't Be Farmed Anymore
- Farmer XP now comes from breaking **fully grown** crops, harvesting ripe bushes and vines by hand (berries, grapes, tomatoes, hops and fruit leaves), and breeding animals.
- These no longer give XP: picking up items (you could drop and re-pick them), sugar cane, and placed pumpkins or melons.
- **Bountiful Harvest** crates now come every **150 Farmer XP**, or every 110 with Season's Blessing, instead of counting picked-up items.

## 🌳 Skill Trees: A Long Road
- Levelling is much slower. You reach half a tree around the end of your first in-game year, and a full tree is a goal that takes several years.
- Nodes are reorganised:
  - Nodes near the start give **items**: tools, bait, seeds, feasts and emeralds.
  - Nodes deep in the tree give **buffs and stats**.
- Node costs now rise with depth.
- Angler XP from drowned and guardians is capped per chunk, so mob farms can't be used to level Angler.

## ⚓ Illager Warships
- Illager Warships now appear about twice as often on the open ocean. Before this, one could appear at most every ~576 blocks, and only if that exact spot was ocean.
